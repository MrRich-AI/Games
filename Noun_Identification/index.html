Noun Identification

What is a noun?

A noun is a word that names a person, a place, a thing, an animal, an idea, or an activity.

teacherperson Mainame parkplace Hanoiplace name bookthing catanimal honestyidea swimmingactivity
Look out for -ing words. When an -ing word names an activity, it is a noun: Swimming is fun. When it shows an action or describes something, it is not: Tom is swimming.

These are not nouns

Action words (run, eat), describing words (red, cool), and small words such as the, on, and, because.

How to play

  1. Read the sentence. There are 10 sentences, from easy (A1) to hard (C1).
  2. Click every noun in the row of word buttons. Click again to un-select a word.
  3. Press Check. You get +1 point for each noun you found and −1 point for each word you picked that is not a noun. A sentence can never score below 0.
  4. After you check, your answer is locked and you can see what was right, wrong, or missed. Press Next to continue.
