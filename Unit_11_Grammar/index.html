Before you begin

In March 1984, a fishing boat capsized off the coast of the Westman Islands, Iceland. Guðlaugur Friðþórsson swam for hours through near-freezing water, then walked barefoot across sharp volcanic rock to find help. Every sentence in this quiz retells a piece of his story — and every answer depends on knowing when to use the past perfect simple (had + past participle) and when to use the past perfect continuous (had been + -ing).
